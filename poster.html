Lead story · Ukraine · War

Russia’s biggest barrage of the month hits Kyiv — and spills into Poland

Hours after Vladimir Putin left the first Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen, Russia fired more than 400 drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine overnight — the largest strike package of November. Eight people were killed, three bridges across the Dnipro in Kyiv were damaged, and power infrastructure was hit in at least 15 regions.

Three Russian objects — a Kh-101 cruise missile and a jet-powered drone among them — crossed into Poland. Warsaw called it a “deliberate provocation”, scrambled Polish and NATO jets, and asked NATO for more air defences.

Markets · Economy

Stocks slide for a seventh straight day as the AI sell-off deepens

Shares fell around the world on Friday. The S&P 500 slid into correction territory, Japan’s Nikkei dropped 5.1%, and chip stocks led the rout as investors questioned sky-high AI valuations.

Washington · Diplomacy

Trump defends his Putin ties; Zelenskyy calls Putin “a killer”

Back from Shenzhen, President Trump defended his relations with Putin and Xi at the White House. Zelenskyy hit back, saying only the US can press Russia — and that such remarks do not help end the war. Trump also signalled no new major strikes on Iran before January.

Europe · Britain & the EU

Second EU–UK summit ends with food, emissions and youth deals

EU leaders hosted Britain’s Andy Burnham in Brussels on Friday for the second EU–UK summit, concluding agreements on food standards, emissions trading and youth mobility as the two sides rebuild ties.

Middle East · Security

Saudi defences down Houthi missile and five drones aimed at Riyadh

Saudi Arabia said it destroyed three Houthi ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh and shot down five drones. Tanker attacks also continued near the Strait of Hormuz as US–Iran talks stalled, with six attacks on shipping reported in a week.
